At Changed, roundups are where most people start — every purchase you make gets rounded up to the nearest dollar, and those cents add up to make extra payments toward your debt. On average, people save around $75 to $100 a month just from roundups. That’s powerful, because it’s money you don’t even miss — but it’s only the beginning.
